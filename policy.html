Privacy Policy

Nathan Gmail Access

This application ("Nathan Gmail Access") is a personal-use automation tool operated solely by its account owner for their own Gmail account. It accesses only that account's email to read, label, organize, and send messages on the owner's own behalf.

No data from this account is shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party, and no data is used to train any AI model beyond the owner's own private automation.

This application is not offered as a public or commercial service and is not available for use by anyone other than its owner.
